Verbalique International exists so that nobody is denied care, justice or opportunity because of the language they speak.

Our story

Verbalique International began with a simple observation: a missed word in a clinic, courtroom or bank can change a life. We set out to build an interpretation service where quality is measured in understanding, not minutes.

Today our interpreters support organizations across Africa and beyond by phone, video and in person, in more than 200 languages and dialects.

What we hold ourselves to

Accuracy first

Every interpreter is tested for language skill and subject vocabulary before taking their first call.

Strict confidentiality

Interpreters sign binding confidentiality agreements, and calls are never recorded without consent.

Respect for people

We interpret faithfully and neutrally, and we train our team in cultural awareness and plain, kind communication.
